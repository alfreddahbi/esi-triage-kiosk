AI Triage Kiosk

Ava will talk with the patient, scan the face continuously, analyze the voice, and combine this with vital signs to suggest an Emergency Severity Index (ESI) level for a nurse to confirm.

Proof of concept. Not a medical device and not for clinical decisions. Use only with staff or simulated patients who agree to camera and microphone use. Images and measurements are sent to the Claude API for analysis and are not stored by this app. A registered nurse must confirm every result.

Best in Chrome or Edge. Allow camera and microphone when asked.
